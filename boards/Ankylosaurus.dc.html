Ankylosaurus

Ankylosaurus magniventris, the fused lizard

Tap the Ankylosaurus to swing its club

A low, broad plant eater covered in bony armour, with horned corners on its skull and a heavy club of bone at the end of a stiff tail.

6–8 m snout to tail tip
70–66 million years ago
60 cm tail club length

The first skeleton was found in 1906 in the Hell Creek Formation of Montana; others come from Wyoming, Alberta and Saskatchewan.
