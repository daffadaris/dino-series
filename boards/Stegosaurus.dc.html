Stegosaurus

Stegosaurus stenops, the narrow-faced roof lizard

Tap the Stegosaurus to swing its tail

A plant eater with two staggered rows of tall bony plates down its back and a spiked tail for defence, carrying its small head about a metre off the ground.

9 m longest individuals
152–145 million years ago
4 tail spikes

Fossils come from the Morrison Formation of Colorado, Wyoming and Utah, with more found in Portugal.
