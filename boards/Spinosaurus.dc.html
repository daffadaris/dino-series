Spinosaurus

Spinosaurus aegyptiacus, the Egyptian spine lizard

Tap the Spinosaurus to make it strike

A long-snouted fish hunter with a tall sail on its back, a deep, paddle-like tail and short hind legs. It stalked the rivers and tidal flats of North Africa.

14 m snout to tail tip
100–94 million years ago
1.65 m longest sail spine

Fossils come from the Kem Kem beds of Morocco and the Bahariya Formation of Egypt, where the first skeleton was found; it was destroyed in 1944.
