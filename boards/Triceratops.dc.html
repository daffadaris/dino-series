Triceratops

Triceratops horridus, the three-horned face

Tap the Triceratops to make it charge

A tank-built plant eater whose head could reach a third of its body length, with a bony frill and three horns. It shared its world with T. rex.

8–9 m snout to tail tip
68–66 million years ago
1 m brow horn length

Fossils are common in the Hell Creek Formation of Montana, Wyoming and the Dakotas, the same rocks that hold T. rex.
