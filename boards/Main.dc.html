T. rex

Tyrannosaurus rex, the tyrant lizard king

Tap the dinosaur to make it roar

One of the largest meat-eating land animals ever, with a bite among the strongest of any land animal.

12 m snout to tail tip
68–66 million years ago
50–60 serrated teeth

Fossils come from the Hell Creek Formation of Montana and the Dakotas, and other Late Cretaceous rocks of western North America.
